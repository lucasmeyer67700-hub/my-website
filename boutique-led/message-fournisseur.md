# Message au fournisseur (AliExpress, en anglais)

À envoyer aux deux vendeurs (masque et lunettes) **et** à un vendeur de secours pour le masque.

---

Hello,

I run an online beauty store in France and I am interested in your product:
[PRODUCT NAME / LINK]

Before ordering samples and starting regular orders, could you please confirm the following:

1. **Dropshipping**: do you accept dropshipping orders (one order = one end customer, shipped to their address)?
2. **Warehouse**: do you have stock in France or in the EU? What is the real delivery time to France (in working days)?
3. **Neutral packaging**: can you ship without any invoice, price, AliExpress flyer or Chinese seller logo in the parcel?
4. **Tracking**: do you provide a tracking number that works on La Poste / Colissimo or 17track within 48 hours after dispatch?
5. **Compliance**: does the device have **CE marking** (and RoHS)? Can you send me the CE certificate / Declaration of Conformity? Is a **French user manual** included in the box?
6. **Specifications**: what are the exact **LED wavelengths** (nm) for each color, the battery capacity and the charging time?
7. **Returns / defects**: what is your policy if a customer receives a defective unit? Do you replace it or refund it?
8. **Volume discount**: what price can you offer from 10, 30 and 50 units per month?

Thank you for your answer. If everything is OK, I will place a first sample order this week.

Best regards,
[YOUR FIRST NAME]

---

## À noter pendant la réponse

- Refuser tout vendeur incapable de fournir une déclaration de conformité CE.
- Garder les captures d'écran des réponses (preuves en cas de litige).
- Lors de la commande d'échantillon : chronométrer le délai réel (commande → réception) et vérifier la notice FR.
